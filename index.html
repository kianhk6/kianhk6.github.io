PAIR: Distance-Preserving Compression for Fast Inference and Adaptation of Vision-Language-Action Policies

IMLE-VLA: Fast Single-Step Action Generation for Vision-Language-Action Policies
Kian Hosseinkhani, Qinhe Peng, George Shramko, Mehran Aghabozorgi, Jianing Qian, Tristan Engst, Alireza Moazeni, Dinesh Jayaraman, Ke Li
IROS 2026

A single-step conditional generator trained with conditional IMLE replaces the iterative flow-matching action head of π0.5: 3.67x higher inference frequency (55 Hz vs. 15 Hz), up to 11x higher action throughput, the highest average success rate on LIBERO (98.0%), and 2.2x to 3.0x lower jerk on a real Franka Emika Panda.
