PAIR: Distance-Preserving Compression for Fast Inference and Adaptation of Vision-Language-Action Policies
Kian Hosseinkhani*, Mehran Aghabozorgi*, Ken Jiankun Zheng†, Kaito Suzuki†, Andy Zhang, Dantong Niu, Trevor Darrell, Kry Yik Chau Lui, Roei Herzig, Ke Li
Under review, 2027

A small trainable compressor shortens a VLA's visual tokens from 512 to 35 while a distance-preservation loss keeps the compressed observations as far apart as the originals: 2.4×–2.8× faster inference with no drop in success on LIBERO and six real-robot tasks, and adaptation time roughly halved.

IMLE-VLA: Fast Single-Step Action Generation for Vision-Language-Action Policies
Kian Hosseinkhani*, Qinhe Peng, George Shramko, Mehran Aghabozorgi, Jianing Qian, Tristan Engst, Alireza Moazeni, Dinesh Jayaraman, Ke Li
IROS 2026

A single-step conditional generator trained with conditional IMLE replaces the iterative flow-matching head of π0.5: 3.67× higher inference frequency, up to 11× action throughput, the top success rate on LIBERO and on every real-robot task, 2.2×–3.0× lower jerk.
